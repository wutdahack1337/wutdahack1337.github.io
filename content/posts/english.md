# Kien Tran method

## Writing (35%)
workflow:
1. read high-quality material - read articles, research papers and essays about different subject
2. study good examples - notice how writers construct sentences, organize paragraphs and support arguments 
3. build your ideas - practice developing arguments, including defending unusual options 
	- try generating ridiculous arguments and then defend them convincingly. your task is to find explanations that make them sound convincing
	- for example: we should not take a shower because... - 1. ancient people didn't take showers regularly, yet they survived; 2. not taking showers saves water; 3. we would need fewer bathrooms
	- it trains: creativity, argument development, persuasive reasoning, the ability to generate ideas under pressure 
4. write regularly - write essays, reports, analyses and other form of English writing
	- use the right words, not difficult words
	- keep your writing concise: avoid unnecessarily long sentences, unnecessary adverbs, cliches such as "it is undeniable that...", announcing your essay with "this essay will discuss..."; make arguments easy to understand
	- prefer objective writing: use expressions such as seems, appears, is likely to, avoiding "I", "we" and "you"
5. check structural mistakes - check sentences structure, topic sentences, logical connections and supporting arguments
6. polish smaller mistakes - correct grammar, articles, tenses, word choice and other errors

## Speaking (25%)
- automate useful expressions - repeat common phases until they come out naturally
- practice thinking - use the 3-minute rule. speak freely without worrying about mistakes
- develop your own ideas - connect topics with your experiences and explain your opinions: it is to learn how to generate ideas from things you already know 
- speak every day - practice alone and talk to other people
- listen frequently - build the listening foundation that supports Speaking

## Listening (20%)
- understand the content
- listen repeatedly
- recognize sounds and expressions naturally

workflow:
1. select one IELTS listening recording with transcript
2. read and understand the transcript
3. underline unfamiliar words and expressions
4. listen to the recording without looking at the transcript
5. replay it repeatedly while doing other activities
6. test yourself again without the transcript
7. move to a new recording while periodically reviewing previous ones

[esl-lab.com](https://www.esl-lab.com) - which provides listening exercises with transcripts, explanations and practical contexts

## Reading (20%)
- Read every day: extensively, not just IELTS passages 
- Understand words through context: annotate directly in your reading material
- Re-read material instead of constantly searching for new passages

workflow:
1. choose interesting material - start with something you can mostly understand
2. read the passage - focus on understanding the overall meaning
3. identify unknow words - don't stop for every unfamiliar word. look up words that prevent you from understanding
4. use search engine - definition -> pronunciation -> synonyms -> images -> how native use the word in real-world contexts
5. annotate the passage - write meanings and synonyms directly beside the relevant words
6. read again - re-read the passage with your newly acquired understanding
7. increase difficulty gradually - move towards more challenging materials as your comprehension improves
