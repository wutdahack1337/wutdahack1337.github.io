the 3 step approach to prepare your speech
1. audience: thinking about the possible interrelationships. For example: do i know them? are they complete strangers? Do I have authority over them? Do they have authority over me? Why I'm speaking about this subject? what does the audience know about the subjects? are they excited by it? are they bored it? are they afraid of it? do they know nothing about it? do they know more than me, less than me? 
2. objective: what is it that i want the audience to do when i'm finished - because whenever you speak, the audience should be changed in some way. So want to be very concrete. Do i want them to take an action? Do i want them to feel an emotion? or do want them to just think differently about something? 
3. message: 2 most important questions of any presentation. What is my key message? What is that one thing that even if the audience forgets everything else, they will take home with them? write the entire presentation in a single sentence. Why should audience care? every speech, every presentation is always about the audience. And if you can't think of a reason why the audience should care then maybe you're speaking to the wrong audience or you need a different speech
